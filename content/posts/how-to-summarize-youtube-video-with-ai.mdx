Long YouTube videos are packed with useful information, but watching them end-to-end takes time. An AI-powered summary lets you extract the key points first, then decide which parts deserve a closer look.

## Why Summarize YouTube Videos?

A 60-minute lecture or interview can contain dozens of ideas, but only a few may be relevant to your current question. Instead of scrubbing through the timeline, a structured summary gives you a map of the entire video in seconds.

Common use cases:

- **Students** reviewing recorded lectures before exams
- **Researchers** scanning expert interviews for specific claims
- **Knowledge workers** catching up on webinars and conference talks
- **Content creators** extracting highlights from long-form podcasts

## How VideoClaw Works

## Tips for Better Results

- **Start with the summary**, then drill into specific sections that matter to you
- **Use transcript search** when you need to find a specific quote or claim
- **Ask follow-up questions** to clarify ideas without leaving the workspace
- **Export subtitles** as SRT or TXT for note-taking or further analysis
