# Video Claw SEO 关键词与 TDH 建议

日期：2026-03-26

品牌信息：

- 名称：`Video Claw`
- 域名：`video-claw.cloud`

参考文档：

- [Video Learning Copilot 产品设计文档](../superpowers/specs/2026-03-23-video-learning-copilot-design.md)

## 1. 核心定位

根据当前产品设计，`Video Claw` 不应被定义成泛化的 `AI video summarizer`，而应被定义为：

`一个可搜索、可追溯、可提问的长视频学习界面`

首页和核心功能页的 SEO 重点，应围绕以下闭环展开：

`关键词检索 -> 精准片段 -> 跳回原视频 -> AI 追问`

## 2. 关键词策略

不建议首页主打：

- `AI video summarizer`
- `YouTube summarizer`
- `summarize long videos`

原因：

- 竞争过于拥挤
- 搜索意图偏向“快速摘要”
- 与产品真正差异化不完全一致

建议优先布局的关键词：

1. `YouTube transcript search`
2. `search inside YouTube videos`
3. `chat with YouTube video`
4. `ask questions about YouTube video`
5. `learn from YouTube videos`

推荐优先级：

- 首页主词：`YouTube transcript search`
- 首页副词：`search inside YouTube videos`
- 功能页主词：`chat with YouTube video`
- 功能页主词：`ask questions about YouTube video`
- 内容页主词：`learn from YouTube videos`

## 3. 首页推荐 TDH

说明：TDH = `Title / Description / H1`

### 推荐版本

- Title: `YouTube Transcript Search & AI Chat for Learning | Video Claw`
- Description: `Paste a YouTube link, search the exact moments you care about, jump to timestamps, and ask AI follow-up questions based on the video transcript.`
- H1: `Search Inside YouTube Videos and Learn Faster`

推荐原因：

- 主关键词靠前，SEO 更直接
- 覆盖了 `transcript search`、`timestamps`、`AI questions` 三类意图
- 与产品“学习优先”而不是“摘要优先”的定位一致

### 品牌感更强的备选版本

- Title: `Video Claw | Search Inside YouTube Videos and Learn Faster`
- Description: `Video Claw helps you search YouTube transcripts, find exact moments, jump to timestamps, and ask AI questions about any video.`
- H1: `Turn YouTube Videos Into Searchable Learning`

## 4. 功能页 TDH 建议

### 页面一：Search Inside YouTube Videos

- 目标关键词：`search inside YouTube videos`
- Title: `Search Inside YouTube Videos and Jump to Exact Moments | Video Claw`
- Description: `Find the exact part of any YouTube video by keyword, open the right timestamp instantly, and keep learning from the original source.`
- H1: `Search Inside Any YouTube Video`

### 页面二：Chat With YouTube Video

- 目标关键词：`chat with YouTube video`
- Title: `Chat With Any YouTube Video Using AI | Video Claw`
- Description: `Ask questions about any YouTube video, get transcript-grounded answers, and jump back to the exact moment for verification.`
- H1: `Chat With Any YouTube Video`

### 页面三：Ask Questions About YouTube Video

- 目标关键词：`ask questions about YouTube video`
- Title: `Ask Questions About Any YouTube Video | Video Claw`
- Description: `Turn long YouTube videos into an interactive learning experience with AI Q&A, timestamped answers, and precise transcript search.`
- H1: `Ask AI Questions About Any YouTube Video`

## 5. 内容页 TDH 建议

### 支柱内容页

- 目标关键词：`learn from YouTube videos`
- Title: `How to Learn From YouTube Videos Without Watching Everything`
- Description: `A practical guide to searching transcripts, finding exact moments, and using AI chat to learn faster from long YouTube videos.`
- H1: `How to Learn From YouTube Videos More Effectively`

这一页适合作为博客或学习中心的支柱内容，用来承接更宽的学习型搜索意图，再导流到产品首页和功能页。

## 6. 品牌层基础文案

- Site name: `Video Claw`
- Domain: `video-claw.cloud`
- Brand tagline: `Search, trace, and learn from long videos.`
- 中文品牌句：`把长视频变成可搜索、可追溯、可提问的学习界面`

## 7. 首页上线建议

如果当前只做一个首页版本，建议直接使用以下组合：

- Title: `YouTube Transcript Search & AI Chat for Learning | Video Claw`
- Description: `Paste a YouTube link, search the exact moments you care about, jump to timestamps, and ask AI follow-up questions based on the video transcript.`
- H1: `Search Inside YouTube Videos and Learn Faster`

这是当前最适合 `Video Claw` 的首页 SEO 方案，因为它同时兼顾：

- 产品差异化
- 英文搜索意图清晰度
- 后续功能页扩展空间
- 品牌识别

## 8. 后续内容扩展方向

首批内容和落地页建议继续围绕以下主题扩展：

- YouTube transcript search
- search inside a video
- ask AI questions about a video
- jump to exact video timestamps
- learn from long videos faster

如果后续要继续扩展 SEO，可以在这份文档基础上补充：

- 首页信息架构
- 首批 10 篇博客选题
- 功能页 URL 规划
- 全站 metadata 模板
